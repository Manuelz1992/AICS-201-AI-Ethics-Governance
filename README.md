# AICS-201: AI Ethics, Governance and Legal Frameworks

## Project Overview

This repository contains my practical assessment of the proposed AI-powered platform developed by SecureFinance Africa, a fictional financial technology organisation.

The assessment examines the ethical, governance, legal and algorithmic fairness implications of using AI for customer identity verification, fraud detection and automated financial risk assessment.

## Assessment Objectives

- Identify ethical and operational AI risks.
- Apply the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
- Examine applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
- Conduct practical algorithmic fairness analysis.
- Evaluate automated decisions using a counterfactual review.
- Recommend safeguards for responsible AI deployment.
